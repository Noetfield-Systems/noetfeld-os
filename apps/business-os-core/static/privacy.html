Privacy notice — Business OS pilot

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Information collected

When you submit an enquiry, we store the name, work email, company, role, audience, selected product/brand, stated problem, urgency, optional estimated opportunity value, consent choices and submission time. Feedback submissions store brand, rating, comment and time.

Why it is used

Information is used to qualify and route the request, create an internal follow-up task, measure the enquiry funnel and improve the relevant product journey. Brand lanes are kept separate in the CRM and memory layer.

AI-assisted processing

Third-party model processing is off unless the optional AI consent box is selected. When selected and a model provider is configured, non-contact request details may be sent to that provider for specialist analysis. Contact names and email addresses are not included in the model prompt. Deterministic routing remains available without AI.

Automated actions

The system creates internal drafts and tasks. It does not automatically send outbound messages, spend money, deploy software, or make legal, investment or binding commercial commitments. Those actions require separate human approval and integrations.

Storage and retention

Enquiry records and operational events are stored in the application database. Requests may be removed by an authorized operator. Do not submit secrets, credentials, sensitive personal information or confidential third-party data.

Contact

For correction or deletion requests, use the same channel through which the pilot link was provided.
